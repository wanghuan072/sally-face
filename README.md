# Sally Face Guide

[Sally Face Guide](https://sallyface.org) is an independent fan-made companion for players exploring the five-part story of *Sally Face*. It is built around the moments players actually search for: a forgotten code, a missable choice, a character connection, an achievement cleanup run, or the official place to buy the game.

## What is Sally Face?

*Sally Face* is a single-player narrative adventure from Portable Moose. It follows Sal Fisher and his friends as an investigation at Addison Apartments grows into a much larger supernatural mystery. The story is told across five connected Chapters, so later events are best experienced in order.

## What this guide covers

- Chapter routes with puzzle clues, codes, key choices and missable moments.
- Character profiles that connect relationships and story appearances.
- All 35 Steam achievements, including hidden and mutually exclusive objectives.
- Focused completion guides for VHS tapes, secrets and replay planning.
- Links to official PC, Android, iOS and console store pages.
- A spoiler-aware page for Sally Face 2's currently confirmed status.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many Chapters are in Sally Face?

The original game tells one continuous story across five Chapters: *Strange Neighbors*, *The Wretched*, *The Bologna Incident*, *The Trial*, and *Memories and Dreams*.

### Should I play the Chapters in order?

Yes. Later Chapters assume knowledge of earlier characters, discoveries and supernatural events. Use a Chapter route after starting the matching save, or return to it during a replay.

### Can every achievement be earned in one run?

No. Some achievements require alternate choices or a Chapter replay. The achievement checklist calls out mutually exclusive and easy-to-miss objectives.

### Where can I get Sally Face on Android?

Use the official Google Play listing linked on the [Download page](https://sallyface.org/download). This site does not host game files, altered packages or standalone APK downloads.
